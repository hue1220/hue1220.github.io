Privacy Policy

Mo Meets the Greats Uploader · Last updated: November 10, 2026

"Mo Meets the Greats Uploader" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Mo Meets the Greats to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Please also see the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the App accesses

  • With the channel owner's permission, the App uploads video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and settings to the owner's own YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • It reads basic information about the owner's own channel and videos, such as video titles, status and view counts, to confirm uploads (youtube.readonly).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload and check videos on the owner's own channel.
  • The App does not collect data from viewers or any other users.
  • The authorization token is stored privately in the channel owner's own Google account storage and is never shared or sold.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, stored tokens no longer work and are deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Mo Meets the Greats channel page.
